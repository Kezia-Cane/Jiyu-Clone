General

How do I make sure I’m buying authentic JiYu?

JiYu products are sold exclusively through jiyuskin.com, Amazon (sold by JiYu Skincare), and TikTok Shop (@jiyuskin). Any other website, third-party seller, or social store claiming to sell JiYu is unauthorized and not affiliated with JiYu in any way.

Unauthorized sellers operate entirely outside our supply chain, which means we have no visibility into what is actually in those products, how they’ve been stored, or whether they meet any safety standard. If you’re unsure whether a seller is legitimate, contact us at support@jiyuskin.com before purchasing.

What makes JiYu different from other skincare brands?

Most K-beauty brands are built around hydration and barrier support. JiYu goes further. We combine Korean formulation precision with longevity-based ingredients that work at the cellular level to repair, rebuild, and extend healthy skin function.

Where are JiYu products made?

Every JiYu product is formulated and manufactured in South Korea at partner facilities operating at the highest standard in the global skincare industry. We manufacture exclusively in South Korea because there is no higher standard anywhere in the world for what we’re building.

Are JiYu products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Our formulas are designed to be gentle. If you’re reactive, we always suggest a patch test first.

How soon can I expect to see results?

Most customers notice healthier glow and smoother texture within 1-2 weeks. Dark spots and fine lines continue to improve with consistent use over 4-8 weeks.

What safety and quality standards do JiYu products meet?

Every JiYu product goes through multiple layers of testing before it ever reaches you. Our manufacturing partners operate under full CGMP, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 22716 cosmetics GMP certification, ECOCERT, and quality control protocols that meet both Korean MFDS and U.S. FDA cosmetic requirements.

Is JiYu a K-beauty brand?

Yes — in every way that matters. JiYu was built on Korean skincare science, formulated by Korean researchers and manufactured in South Korea at one of the world’s leading cosmetic facilities.

What are longevity-based ingredients and why do they matter?

Longevity-based ingredients come from longevity medicine research, the science of what keeps cells healthy, functional, and resilient over time. They support how skin repairs, regenerates, and ages from within, which means results compound instead of fading quickly.

Usage

How do I use the Toning Polish Pads?

After cleansing, swipe one pad gently across the face and neck, avoiding the eye area. Do not rinse — let the essence absorb, then follow with serum and moisturizer/SPF.

How often should I use them?

Use twice daily — morning and night — for best results.

Should I rinse after using the pads?

No. Leave the essence on to fully absorb.

Can I use the pads with my current skincare routine?

Absolutely. They’re designed to fit seamlessly with your routine.

What step of my skincare routine should they be used in?

Right after cleansing and before serums, moisturizer, and SPF.

How long does one jar last?

Each jar contains 100 pads.

  • Twice daily (2 pads/day): about 50 days
  • Once daily (1 pad/day): about 100 days
Can I use the pads before applying makeup?

Yes — the essence leaves skin smooth, hydrated, and foundation-friendly.

Ingredients & Safety

What is K8-Rejuvenate™ and how does it work?

K8-Rejuvenate™ is our proprietary complex of 8 clinically backed actives — including niacinamide, snail mucin, peptides, Centella, vitamin C, alpha-arbutin, licorice root, and maltobionic acid — designed to brighten, smooth, hydrate, and visibly improve tone and texture.

What are the main ingredients in JiYu products?

Niacinamide, Snail Mucin, Peptide Complex, Centella Asiatica, Vitamin C (3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid), Alpha-Arbutin, Licorice Root Extract, Maltobionic Acid, Hyaluronic Acid, and soothing humectants.

Are the ingredients really sourced from Korea?

Yes — our ingredients and manufacturing are proudly Korean.

Are JiYu products cruelty-free or vegan?

We are cruelty-free. Some formulas contain snail mucin, so not all products are vegan.

Do JiYu products contain parabens, sulfates, or alcohol?

Our formulas are free from parabens, sulfates, and phthalates. We do not use drying alcohols.
