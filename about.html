Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer a Money Back Guarantee?

Yes — try it risk-free for 90 days. If you’re not in love, we’ll refund you.

How does the subscription work?

Choose your cadence (every 30, 60, or 90 days) and save automatically. You’re in control — update anytime from your account.

How do I use the JiYu Toning Polish Pads?

After cleansing, swipe one pad gently across the face and neck, avoiding the eye area. Do not rinse — let the essence absorb, then follow with serum and moisturizer/SPF.

How often should I use them?

Use twice daily — morning and night — for best results.

Are JiYu products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Our formulas are designed to be gentle. If you’re reactive, we always suggest a patch test first.

Where are JiYu products made?

Every JiYu product is formulated and manufactured in South Korea at partner facilities operating at the highest standard in the global skincare industry.
